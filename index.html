How to Use Video Builder

Everything you need to know to create, edit, and share AI-generated demo videos.

1

Create a New Video

Click "+ New Video" on the Videos page. Give your video a name, pick a brand, and paste in a PocketSIC project URL. Choose your narration voice and target duration, then hit "Create Video". The AI will generate a narration script from your demo script and begin producing the video.

2

Watch the Pipeline Work

Once you kick off generation, the pipeline status card shows each step in real time — scripting, voiceover, scene capture, compositing, and upload. Each step lights up as it completes so you always know where things stand. The full process typically takes a few minutes depending on video length.

3

Review Your Video

When the video is ready, it appears in the built-in player at the top of the detail page. Below that you'll find the full narration script broken down by segment — each row shows the segment type (intro, scene capture, b-roll, transition, outro), narration text, and timing. You can download the MP4 directly from the player controls.

4

Edit Segments Inline

Click the pencil icon on any segment row to edit its narration text directly. You can adjust the wording, fix phrasing, or completely rewrite the narration for any individual segment. Changes are saved instantly so you can fine-tune the script without regenerating the entire video.

5

Regenerate Individual Scenes

Not happy with a specific b-roll clip or scene capture? Use the regenerate button on any segment to re-generate just that piece — new b-roll footage, a fresh screen capture, or updated voiceover. You can also regenerate the entire video with updated settings if you want a complete refresh.

6

Update Video Settings

On the detail page, expand the Video Settings panel to change the name, brand, narration voice, target duration, background music track, or add custom instructions. A yellow "You have unsaved changes" banner will appear with a Save button so you never lose track of what's changed.

7

Share with a Public Link

Toggle the Public Player Page switch in the settings to generate a shareable link and QR code. Anyone with the link can watch the video in a clean, branded player page. You can optionally add password protection so only people with the password can view it — perfect for sharing with customers or partners.

8

Download & Present

Click the Download button to grab the finished MP4 with branded intro, narrated scenes, b-roll, and background music — ready to present at customer meetings, embed in presentations, or share on Slack. Each video is a self-contained story that showcases the Agentic Enterprise powered by Salesforce.
